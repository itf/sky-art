# Constellation art for the wrap-around sky puzzles

These are the constellation illustrations by **Johan Meuris** from
[Stellarium](https://stellarium.org)'s "modern" sky culture. They have been
modified for use as a faint picture layer on wrap-around sky jigsaw puzzles.

## Files

### `constellation-art-whole-sky.png`

8192 × 4096 pixels, transparent background.

- Tinted a soft blue-white. The drawing's brightness becomes its opacity.
- Faded to 22% opacity.
- Each picture is pinned to the sky by its three anchor stars and bent onto an
  equirectangular map of the whole sky:
  - right ascension runs across (360°, 0h at the left edge),
  - declination runs down (+90° at the top to −90° at the bottom).

### `constellation-art-sky-110-28x18cm-board.png`

3482 × 2229 pixels, 300 dpi, transparent background.

The same layer, wrapped exactly as the 110-piece wrap-around sky jigsaw puzzle
(28.5 × 17.9 cm) prints it. It is bent onto the puzzle's layout (Lee's
tetrahedral projection), with each piece's art where that piece is printed on
the sheet. On the puzzle itself the sky, stars, lines and labels are painted
together with this layer, and the colours of the whole picture are adjusted
afterwards.
